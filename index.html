PLAYSTYLE ARCHETYPES

A LOOK AT CLASSES

A rating tells you how a player scores. A class describes how they play. Some hold their ground, some keep moving, and others try something you did not see coming.

Explore the classes ↗

Weaver

Uses close control and changes of direction to get around opponents.

Bulwark

Holds their ground to protect possession and block dangerous routes.

Catalyst

Starts moves and puts opponents under pressure to make something happen.

Tactician

Reads the play and gets into position before the next touch.

Opportunist

Takes advantage of loose balls, mistakes, and brief openings.

Dynamo

Keeps moving and stays involved from one challenge to the next.

Renegade

Tries unexpected touches and approaches to create their own openings.

THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE RATINGS

BEHIND THE SCENES

Admins put the assessments together, keep player records up to date, and add notes to explain the ratings.

Putting an assessment together

Admins rate six attributes and describe the player's style, strengths, and areas to improve. A new player's overall tier is calculated from their average attribute score.

Working on drafts

A draft can be saved before it is ready. Other admins can review it, while its author can continue editing and push it to the public player list.

Keeping a record

Assessments include the admin who saved them. The admin activity log records changes to existing assessments, including the previous and updated values.

Administrator tools are available through the Admin link.
